What a report does and does not say

It states

  • Units are integer grams. Configured process loss is explicit per transformation.
  • Mixed inputs propagate conservatively: every descendant lot is treated as wholly affected.
  • Every contributing path, with the source row behind each event.
  • Rejected imports never produce a partial graph; the prior accepted batch stays, labeled.

It does not state

  • Any contamination probability or affected share of a blend. Lots are flagged whole.
  • That customers absent from a report are safe. They are outside the trace, not certified. No notifications are sent.
  • Any recall, safety, FDA or FSMA determination. No regulatory rule mapping is done.
  • Anything about real customers, facilities or datasets. Every record here is synthetic and fictional.
