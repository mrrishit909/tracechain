What a report does and does not say

  • Units are integer grams. Configured process loss is explicit per transformation.
  • Mixed inputs propagate conservatively: every descendant lot is treated as wholly affected.
  • Rejected imports never produce a partial graph; the prior accepted batch stays, labeled.
  • Customers absent from a report are not certified safe. No notifications are sent.
